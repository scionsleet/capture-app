Capture

Capture now. Revisit by day. Never organize.

Capture opens on a blank page with the keyboard up. Type a thought, tap the blue arrow, and it's saved to today. Swipe back to see any earlier day. No folders, tags or titles.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: open an issue on GitHub.

Tips

  • ⌘↩ sends on Mac and with a hardware keyboard.
  • Type “- ” to start a bullet list.
  • Tap any past thought to edit it; empty it to delete it.
  • Your thoughts sync between your iPhone and Mac through your own iCloud account.
