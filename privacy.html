Privacy Policy

Capture: Thought Inbox · Updated November 8, 2026

Capture does not collect any data. The developer has no servers and never receives, sees or stores anything you write.

Where your thoughts live

Everything you type is stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, it is also stored in your own private iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage) so it can sync between your iPhone and Mac. That data is governed by Apple's privacy policy and is not accessible to the developer.

What Capture doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising
  • No tracking, and no data shared with or sold to anyone

Deleting your data

Delete any thought in the app, or delete the app. To remove the synced copy, delete the app from all your devices; you can also manage iCloud storage in Settings.
